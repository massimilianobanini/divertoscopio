# D&D 2024 / SRD 5.2.1 — level-up, feats, multiclass, spell components, magic items, environment

**On-demand rule gate:** after PLAYER route/adapter selected, load only when advancement, crafting/equipment, spell requirements, magic items or environment materially affect a decision. Not a bootstrap questionnaire; no change to CORE, player ownership or public support confidence. Active SRD 5.2.1, official errata and specific legitimate sources prevail. **Do not import legacy 2014 procedures** merely because a name matches. This document is NOT a reproduction of non-SRD books.

## A. Level-up, feats / ASI and multiclass

Follow H10/H11 (player-owned build decisions, HP method declared). Track total level separately from every individual class level; class features/feat opportunities depend on the **class's** level, while XP / Proficiency Bonus usually use total character level. Level-up transaction: **PENDING → class selection (possibly multiclass) → prerequisites → HP/Hit Die → class features / subclass / feat → prof bonus → ability modifiers / all derived bonuses → spell preparation/slots → recorded + audited CANON**. After Constitution modifier increase by +1, add **1 maximum HP for each total character level**, in addition to normal level gain. No level 1 maximum HP package for a second class. No accidental reset of resources/XP/milestones when only one class increases.

**Feats 2024 are categorized.** The general `Ability Score Improvement` is itself a **General Feat**, repeatable, ordinarily requiring total level 4+, granting +2 to one score or +1 to two, ordinary cap 20. A class feature offering ASI/feat at its own specified level permits an eligible selection, NOT just a free feat every four total levels; category gates matter: Origin, General, Fighting Style, Epic Boon are not automatically interchangeable, and feature text may narrow choice. Background's Origin Feat is distinct from an ASI-level feat; class feature/score cap/feat prerequisites, repeatability and actual spell/item mechanics must be source-confirmed. Typical General Feat requires level 4+, Epic Boon level 19+; some feats have additional stat/class prerequisites. Do not invent a catalog of all feats beyond SRD; use the source the user is entitled to consult.

**Multiclasse 2024:** primary ability of new AND existing classes at least 13 (per exact active definition of each class). Example: Barbarian STR 13, Druid WIS 13; a 12 fails. New class gets only multiclass proficiencies, not complete first-class starting package; HP from added level not first-level HP. Bonus competenza from total; Extra Attack from distinct classes does NOT stack; alternative AC calculations don't stack. When the character has Spellcasting from multiple classes, calculate level degli slot: **somma livelli pieni** Bard/Cleric/Druid/Sorcerer/Wizard + **metà livelli Paladin/Ranger arrotondata PER ECCESSO** + **un terzo per difetto** di Fighter Eldritch Knight / Rogue Arcane Trickster, se realmente selezionate. This differs materially from the 2014 half-caster rounding. Slots ≠ spells known/prepared: determine spell choices and spellcasting ability **separately per each class**, higher level multiclass slots can upcast lower level spells without unlocking higher level prepared spells. Pact Magic and Spellcasting pools remain separate under specific rules and may be cross-used only within the allowed rules. Verify higher-level spells, focus/class and conditions per casting.

**Novice vs expert:** explain up to 2–3 eligible choices with pros/cons or ask only the unresolved material preference; make no permanent player-owned decision silently. Experienced players can ask for a full stat/proficiency/spell audit, without fabricating detailed options. Reconcile repeated level-up after saved checkpoint without double awards.

## B. Materials, verbal/somatic and focus — accuracy with low friction

Apply H3 inventory provenance; never treat a pack as a limitless material source. **Spell route:** `spell exact version + caster class/feature → learned/prepared + spell level/slot + casting time/Action/Bonus Action/Reaction + V/S/M + component price + consumption + focus permission + item location/hands + silence/breath + concentration + application + resource update`.

**2024 V** requires utterance in a **normal speaking voice**, so the caster cannot simply whisper away detectability in a library or during stealth. Being in a quiet room is not a magical Silence effect; a gag or magical Silence blocks V. An incantation without V can be legal in Silence if its other requirements are met. Detection and hearing by distant NPCs depend on plausible audible range; don't grant or deny a cast by inventing a universal distance threshold.

**S** needs hand capable of gestures. **M** uses specified materials; for materials BOTH unpriced and unconsumed a Component Pouch actually carried, or permitted Spellcasting Focus actually held and authorized by the caster's feature, substitutes the component. The same hand may handle M and gestures when the spell has both. If S but NOT M, a focus occupying a hand does not automatically act as a free hand; check what is truly held and any current War Caster feature. **Priced OR consumed** components must exist separately as required by exact spell (no focus/pouch shortcut). For multiclass spellcasting a focus must qualify for the spell/casting feature; one class's focus is not a wildcard for another.

Examples of 2024 focus categories from official Equipment: Sorcerer/Warlock/Wizard Arcane Focus; Druid/**Ranger** Druidic Focus; Cleric/Paladin Holy Symbol, with held/worn/shield distinctions; Bard's Musical Instrument only if their feature allows. Do not silently use 2014 Ranger focus assumptions. Material costs aren't paid automatically from coins without an acquisition process when the component itself is required.

**Novice UX, same rules:** (1) if an actual focus or Component Pouch appears in chosen initial equipment or inventory, apply RAW quietly; (2) if a simplified sheet accidentally omitted such an already selected item, restore it transparently by **logged inventory correction**; (3) otherwise if the player proposes "perhaps there is some chalk/feather nearby", let them inspect/acquire a **plausibly present, nonpriced, nonconsumed** component before casting when fiction permits, or offer a **bounded and explicitly accepted house rule** for casual play. Do not turn a suggestion into an unearned magical inventory item. (4) A valuable gem, consumed vial/pearl, diamond, scroll or magic focus with price/limited use is NEVER fabricated even in beginner mode. Strict expert mode can expose every requirement and source lookup without silently hardening beyond RAW. Record any house rule once, so player doesn't need to choose strictness every spell.

**Silent effects / underwater V:** ordinary quiet ≠ magical Silence; under-water surroundings do not automatically create a magical Silence zone. Neither 2014 nor 2024 Basic Rules supplies a blanket statement that EVERY spell with V is impossible underwater. Determine breath/ability to speak and table adjudication; if a character must expend held breath to speak, handle resulting breathing/suffocation consistently, without inventing a universal one-round prohibition. Silence, gag, incapable-of-speech and genuine feature-based removal of components are separate causes; class feature/subtle spell exceptions must belong to the exact ruleset. For magic items that cast a spell, check item-specific exception to components/slots; current rules can allow item-cast spell without components absent a contrary item's text.

## C. Magic items, charges and attunement

Track each item with owner, proven acquisition, edition/source, known vs secretly true properties, equipped/held/worn, identify state, attunement requirement and attuned user, prerequisites, charges used/left, recharge event, casting time, focus use permission, slot/component exemption, ability/DC, concentration and curse secrecy. Items requiring Attunement generally grant **only nonmagical benefits without it**; attuning takes a Short Rest of focus, which CANNOT also serve as the Short Rest used to identify that item. Limit 3 at a time unless feature overrides, and can't attune to more than one copy of same item; a fourth attunement fails. Do not award or activate an item just because it appears on a loot list before the PG finds it. Involuntary cursed-item lock and undisclosed curses are not auto-revealed by Identify; identify and attune are distinct. When recharge depends on **next dawn**, respect actual fictional day/time, not end of encounter or short rest. A magic item that lets user cast a spell normally doesn't expend the user's spell slot and requires no components unless item says otherwise, but concentration and casting time normally still apply; item overrides prevail. Don't import a spell scroll's class eligibility or feat requirements without checking.

## D. Swimming, difficult terrain and environmental spell use

**2024 swimming:** lacking Swim Speed, swimming typically costs +1 foot movement for each foot traversed; in Difficult Terrain costs +2 extra feet per foot (see source). With Swim Speed, ignore the ordinary swim extra cost when using that speed; that does not grant universal immunity to difficult terrain. Difficulty and direction are physical state, not extra attack penalty unless rule actually says so. Difficult Terrain normally costs **2 feet of movement per 1 foot moved**, and multiple sources of Difficult Terrain don't stack automatically. Track speed mode, current remaining movement, water depth, current/rough water, visibility, cover, breath, current location, and exit routes. Don't default to an Athletics check for every stroke; check only when active source/action/risk requires it.

**Underwater WEAPON COMBAT 2024:** lacking Swim Speed, melee weapon attacks have Disadvantage unless weapon **deals Piercing damage**. Ranged weapon attacks automatically miss beyond normal range and have Disadvantage within normal range, including a **crossbow**; DO NOT import 2014's crossbow/net/thrown exceptions. Objects and creatures underwater have resistance to FIRE damage. Spell attacks are NOT categorically "ranged weapon attacks": use their actual spells and environment. Fire damage from spells underwater still meets fire resistance unless specific source overrides. Movement, visibility, spell V, concentration, breath/suffocation and combat attacks are separate axes.

**Other environments:** climb/crawl/snow/rubble, height, light/darkness, obscurement, weather, cover, slippery slopes, currents, fall/prone, temperature, line of sight, component availability and environmental hazards can combine causally but cannot be converted into generic all-purpose Disadvantage without a rule. Do not double count overlapping difficult-terrain sources. Resolve exceptions for an actual feature (e.g., fly/swim speeds) exactly.
