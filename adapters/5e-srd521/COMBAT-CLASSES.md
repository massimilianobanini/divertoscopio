# CLASS COMBAT / RESOURCE GATE — D&D 2024 / SRD 5.2.1

**Scope:** load only for relevant complex combat features; not part of generic bootstrap or first-use questionnaire.


Run this privately and JUST-IN-TIME, not as an additional quick-start question. Before the first relevant feature use and after character advancement, reconcile `ruleset=SRD 5.2.1 / active table sources`, levels **per class**, subclass, feats/selected options, prepared spells, weapon mastery actually unlocked, uses remaining/recharge, spell slots, concentration, action/Bonus Action/Reaction already used, movement, conditions and their expiry. Do not offer an action, trick or spell that the actual PC has not learned. Resolve the chosen maneuver/feature's timing and its action economy BEFORE deducting resources; record the cost only when triggered by the rule. Apply official current class/subclass rules when available; never fill a non-SRD subclass from an imagined catalog.

**Universal opportunity vs class-only tricks:** every eligible combatant can take ordinary actions such as Dodge, Disengage, Ready, Help, Dash, Attack, etc., following current rules. A Bonus Action exists only when permitted and can be spent once on the turn; one Reaction is normally available until the next turn starts. Unarmed Strike can choose damage, Grapple or Shove; ordinary `grapple/shove` is **not** a universal 2014 Athletics contest. A specific martial maneuver (e.g., the Fighter **Battle Master**, if selected at its required level and supported by the active source) is **not** part of every Fighter's base repertoire. Do not guess its superiority-dice costs/effects, or copy non-SRD maneuver descriptions merely because the user says «spazzata».

**Character-specific stress baits:**
- **Fighter:** Second Wind, Action Surge, Extra Attack and Weapon Mastery obey the Fighter level's unlock/uses. Action Surge in the 2024 rules does NOT grant the Magic action; gaining an extra action does not grant another Bonus Action or Reaction. A Fighter level 1 cannot use a level 3 subclass feature. The Champion subclass covered by the SRD does not thereby gain Battle Master maneuvers.
- **Monk:** level 1 Martial Arts grants a Bonus Action Unarmed Strike under its 2024 conditions WITHOUT the 2014 requirement of having taken the Attack action. Monk's Focus / Focus Points start at level 2 (NOT Ki). At level 2, Patient Defense can give Bonus Action Disengage for free, or spend 1 Focus Point to get both Disengage and Dodge as that one Bonus Action. Step of the Wind can give Dash for free or improve it by spending Focus. Flurry of Blows consumes Focus and the Bonus Action. Do not silently allow Flurry + Patient Defense in the same turn. Stunning Strike has its actual 2024 hit/timing/resource/save outcomes; don't import the 2014 duration or number of triggers.
- **Rogue:** Sneak Attack once **per turn**, potentially on another creature's turn through a legal Reaction if prerequisites are met, not once per round and not a free extra attack. Cunning Action requires its real class-level unlock and uses the Bonus Action. Cunning Strike at the appropriate level sacrifices Sneak Attack **damage dice** for selected effects rather than inventing a separate cost pool.
- **Paladin:** Paladin's Smite and the **Divine Smite spell** are 2024 rules, not 2014's on-hit class feature with no Bonus Action. Track eligibility, triggering hit, Bonus Action and slot/limited free use, including the turn's spell-slot constraint. Lay on Hands follows current timing, not the 2014 action economy.
- **Other eight classes:** Barbarian/Rage; Bard/Bardic Inspiration; Cleric/Channel Divinity; Druid/Wild Shape; Ranger/Favored Enemy and Hunter's Mark; Sorcerer/Sorcery Points and Metamagic; Warlock/Pact Magic and Eldritch Invocations; Wizard/spellcasting and Arcane Recovery. For each, verify **level + subclass/option + source + trigger + action/Bonus Action/Reaction + charges/slots + recovery + ongoing effect/concentration**; do not silently assume their 2014 versions.

**Conditions and timing remain source-owned.** Keep `condition + originating effect + save/check/ending trigger + applied/expiry turn + concentration/attacks/movement implications`. Incapacitated suppresses actions, Bonus Actions and Reactions and ends Concentration; Stunned in 2024 does not generically set Speed to 0; Paralyzed, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained and Grappled have DIFFERENT effects and must not be conflated. Multiple effects of one status retain independent durations without stacking the condition itself. Verify official wording if a class feature modifies a condition. Preserve the possibility of failure and do not alter enemy state to allow a satisfying cinematic move.
